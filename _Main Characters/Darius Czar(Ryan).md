## Professor Wood
Given after **Charm** Roll(9) **ES** 
After sending letters to several of London's best Universities you've gotten a serious response from one. Professor Wood from King's College London. He said he might have something interesting for you. You travel to the university and meet Wood in his lecture hall. You can see some Latin words on the blackboard, you can read most of it, you're unsure if he will be able to teach you anything new. After a short conversation it's clear he is willing to connect you to someone else. A priest in the Vatican who still speaks a more traditional Latin. Wood does ask for one thing in return for connecting you to him, you must deliver a package to him when you go to see him. Do you accept?
