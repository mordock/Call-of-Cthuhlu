These time powers are connected to the [[Yith]], they have these powers by default and can give them to other beings. 
Having time means you are functionally immortal, meaning you'll stop aging and you'll stay who you are forever for so long as you have time. 
Time will slowly tick down and when it reaches 0 you instantly die. 

There's a few ways to get your time to go up:
Alpha radiation (Don't ask why, it just works)
Getting it from someone else (other people or Yith)

When they get their time they will roll for their time
6D6 for hours(Gilbert 12D6)
6D10 for minutes
6D8 for seconds
